01 — Who I am

A student of systems.

Pakistan ICS

I'm an Intermediate in Computer Science (ICS) student based in Pakistan. My pull toward technology has always been about understanding — how something works beneath the surface, why a system behaves the way it does, and what becomes possible once you actually understand it.

Most of what I know came from independent exploration rather than a syllabus: reading documentation, breaking things, rebuilding them, and following curiosity into unfamiliar territory. AI, programming, cybersecurity and physics each offer a different angle on the same underlying question — how do complex systems actually work?

I'd rather build a small, imperfect project that teaches me something real than read about one that doesn't. That's the pattern that has stuck: learn by building, understand by experimenting, and keep going.
